Data notes
Revenue is ex-VAT and net of discounts, counting only completed, non-voided sales — the same basis as the Lightspeed sales report.
Includes ZAR 1,204,318 of revenue (284 lines, ZAR 402,140 gross profit) from Adjustments.xlsx — sales made outside Lightspeed. Lightspeed alone accounts for ZAR 37,208,587, the figure that ties to the Lightspeed report.
ZAR 3,358,492 of revenue (8.7%) has no unit cost on record — labour and service lines legitimately carry no COGS, which is why Service Centre, Paarl Trails and Retul show margins near 100%.
Includes 612 return/refund lines totalling ZAR −418,204, netted against revenue. Line-level and sale-level revenue agree to within ZAR 0. Stock turn is a proxy: COGS ÷ closing stock at cost.
